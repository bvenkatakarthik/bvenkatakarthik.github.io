Speculation. 

How did we learn as infants? 

$${ \boxed{ \text{Neurons that fire together wire together.} } }$$ 

$${ \boxed{ \text{We learn by forming associations.} } }$$ 

We learn by a combination of:
* innate curiosity, 
* innate drives, 
* forming associations,
* observing and mimicking the people around us. 

We do the same as adults at a subconscious level. 

**Eg**: We are able to experience life because of neurons firing, and the innate and formed associations between things in the material world and our mental experiences.    
We have some control over neurons firing, and forming associations.
