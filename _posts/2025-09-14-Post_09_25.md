Speculation. 

How did we learn as infants? 

Neurons that fire together wire together. We learn by association.

We learn by a combination of:
* innate curiosity, 
* innate drives, 
* forming associations,
* observing and mimicking the people around us. 

We do the same as adults at a subconscious level. 

**Eg**: We are influenced by reading, and other activities where we can form associations. By reading we can associate the words with experiences.

**Eg**: People keep lucky objects. They associate the lucky object with certain good outcomes.
