Speculation. 

How did we learn as infants? 

$${ \boxed{ \text{Neurons that fire together wire together.} } }$$ 

$${ \boxed{ \text{We learn by forming associations.} } }$$ 

We learn by a combination of:
* innate curiosity, 
* innate drives, 
* forming associations,
* observing and mimicking the people around us. 

We do the same as adults at a subconscious level. 

**Eg**: We are able to experience life because of innate and formed associations. 

**Eg**: We are able to read because of formed associations.

**Eg**: People keep lucky objects. Here we learn to associate the lucky object with us achieving certain good outcomes.
