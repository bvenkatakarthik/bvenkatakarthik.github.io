[**Getting Started**] 

[Back to top](#top).

A computer does two things, and two things only: 

* It performs calculations. 

* It remembers the results of those calculations. 

But it does those two things extremely well. 

The typical computer performs a 100 billion or so calculations a second. 

As for memory, a small computer might have hundreds of gigabytes of storage.
