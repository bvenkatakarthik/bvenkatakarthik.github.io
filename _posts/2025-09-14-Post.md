Speculation. 

How did we learn as infants? 

We learn by a combination of:
* innate curiosity, 
* innate drives, 
* mimicking the people around us. 

We do the same as adults at a subconscious level. 

**Eg**: "A good stack of examples, as large as possible, is indispensable for a thorough understanding of any concept, and when I want to learn something new, I make it my first job to build one." - Halmos.
