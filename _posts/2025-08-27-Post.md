Speculation. 

1)

How did we learn as infants? 

We learn by a combination of:
* innate curiosity, 
* innate drives, 
* mimicking the people around us. 

We do the same as adults at a subconscious level. 

Hence the concept of an anthropomorphic god can be a useful mental device. 

2)

[Hebb's rule] 

We also learn by association. Neurons that fire together wire together.
