$${ \underline{\textbf{Reading List}} }$$ 

Inquiry based approach. 

**Mathematics and Data Science**
* ["Foundations of Applied Mathematics" by Humpherys, Jarvis](https://foundations-of-applied-mathematics.github.io)
* “Undergraduate Algebra” and “Undergraduate Analysis” by Lang.  
* “Introductory Real Analysis” by Kolmogorov, Fomin.
* "Modern Geometry" by Dubrovin, Fomenko, Novikov. 
* “A Probability Path” by Resnick.
* “Theory of Statistics” by Schervish. 
* “Numerical Optimization” by Nocedal, Wright. 
* [“Programming in Python” by Sedgewick, Wayne, Dondero](https://introcs.cs.princeton.edu/python/home/)
* [Think Series by Downey](https://greenteapress.com/wp/) 
* “Data Science from Scratch” by Grus. 
* ["An Introduction to Statistical Learning" by Hastie, Tibshirani](https://www.statlearning.com)
* “Inference and Learning from Data” by Sayed. 

**Computer Science**
* [CS50 by Malan](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2025/)
* [Teach Yourself CS](https://teachyourselfcs.com/) 
* “The C Programming Language” by Kernighan, Ritchie. 
* "C Programming" by King. 
* ["Foundations of Computer Science" by Aho, Ullman](http://infolab.stanford.edu/~ullman/focs.html) 
* “Algorithms in C” by Sedgewick. 

**Physics**
* "Mathematics for Physics" by Stone, Goldbart.
* "Theoretical Physics" by Joos.
* "A complete course on Theoretical Physics" by Lindner, Strauch.
* "Berkeley Physics Course (in SI units)".       
* [The Feynman Lectures on Physics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu) 
* "Course of Theoretical Physics" by Landau, Lifshitz.
