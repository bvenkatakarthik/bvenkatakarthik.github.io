The foundational idea behind money seems to be: 

Be of “value” to others, by involving in selling something. Earn “value” in return, by earning money. 

One problem is: This “value” earned, namely money, is often unequally distributed. 

Another problem is: "Value" to other people isn't the only measure of value. For eg, Scientific Discoveries which improve Knowledge but don't translate into direct value for other people. (I guess Research is often treated as a Public Good by Economists. Link to article: [Link](https://www.uaf.edu/news/research-as-a-public-good.php)). 

Is there a simple solution to economic inequality people are missing?

To study: Countries with less economic inequality, like Norway and Finland.
