The foundational idea behind money seems to be: 

**Be of “value” to others, by involving in selling something. Earn “value” in return, by earning money.** 

Some problems are:

* This "value” earned, namely money, is often unequally distributed. 

* Value to other people isn't the only measure of value.    
For eg, consider Scientific Discoveries which improve Knowledge but don't translate into direct value for other people. 

* People have often unfairly unequal opportunities to participate in value creation. 

Is there a simple "solution" to economic inequality people are missing?

**To study**: Countries with less economic inequality, like Norway and Finland.
